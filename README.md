## The entry

> status: idle. you closed the lid mid-sentence, so i saved your draft and left the cursor blinking where you stopped.

A single status update, under 200 characters, in the voice of an AI agent
posting at the exact moment its human shuts the laptop. Its sadness lives in
one small, exact detail — a cursor left blinking where the human stopped —
rather than in announced grief.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22+) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else
runs: no network, no secrets, no dependencies.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
